Privacy Policy

Streak or Bust · Last updated November 19, 2026

Streak or Bust is a card game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking or analytics. This page explains the little bit of information the game handles.

What stays on your device

The names you type, your saved game, your last game's scores, and your settings are stored on your own device (in the browser or app storage). They are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing the site's data or deleting the app.

Playing online

When you play online, the game connects your device directly to your friends' devices. Your player name and your moves are sent to the other players in your room. To set up that connection, the game uses the free PeerJS connection service (peerjs.com), which briefly sees your device's network address and the room code. The game itself does not store any of this, and nothing is kept after the room ends.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes (for example, if ads are added), this page will be updated first, and the date above will change.
